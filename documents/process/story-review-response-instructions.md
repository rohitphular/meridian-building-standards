# Story Review Response Instructions

The desk responds to each finding raised in the story spec review. Every finding gets a resolution. No finding is left unaddressed.

---

## For Each Finding

**Fixed** — describe what was changed and where in the story spec. Be specific: section name, what was added or corrected.

**Rejected** — state why the finding is incorrect. Cite the spec section that supports the current wording. A rejection without a cited reason will be re-raised.

**Deferred** — state why it cannot be resolved now and when it will be. Deferred items become Carry-Forward in the next review round.

---

## Rules

- Address every New Finding and every Carry-Forward from the review feedback. Do not skip items.
- Update the story spec file before writing the response — the response describes changes already made, not planned changes.
- If a finding requires a design decision, make the decision and record it in the story spec before responding.
- Do not respond with "will fix" — fix it first, then respond.
- Complete the pre-submission checklist in the story creation instructions after making changes.

---

## Disputed Rejections

If the reviewer disagrees with a rejection in the subsequent round, they re-raise it in Carry-Forward as "Disputed — rejection not accepted" with a counter-argument. After two consecutive rounds of dispute on the same finding, the scrum master arbitrates. The arbitration decision is final for that story.
